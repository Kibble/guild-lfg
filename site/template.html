Install in three steps

  1. Download & extractClose the game and extract your chosen ZIP. Inside is the GuildQueue folder.
  2. Copy the addonPut that folder in your Forever installation's Interface/AddOns directory.
  3. Enable & playEnable Guild Queue in the AddOns list, then type /gq in game.

Use the same addon version as your guildmates. Updating preserves saved preferences; join the queue again after login.

Guild-only coordination. Invitations are always manual. No automatic accepting or teleporting.
